Sources checked 9 September 2026
01 / METHOD

Count within a dataset.

Population results sum published age cells for the selected sex and geography. Relationship results sum selected status cells within one published age band. We do not multiply unrelated national averages.

The percentage always names its denominator. Male and female counts come from their own tables; “All people” uses the published persons table.

02 / UNCERTAINTY

Precision has limits.

These are official estimates, not a live census or a prediction of dating success. Relationship figures use survey data. Source values include their published confidence intervals and reliability codes.

We do not combine cell confidence intervals into a new interval without covariance information. Headline rounding does not express a confidence interval.

03 / COVERAGE

Geography matters.

The UK population snapshot covers all four countries and English regions. The relationship dataset covers England and Wales only, with different age bands and a different reference year.

The app keeps those datasets separate. It does not project England-and-Wales relationship rates onto Scotland or Northern Ireland.

What happened to the other filters? Evidence review ↓

The earlier calculator contained figures without an auditable extraction or a compatible denominator. Those figures are preserved in the legacy code, but excluded from this version’s results.
