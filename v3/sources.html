How to trace a result

The calculator breakdown shows the selected values, the reduction at each step and the workbook cells used. Download a result for the full age-by-sex calculation. This library explains each dataset’s coverage and use.

Official counts, survey proportions and modelling assumptions are different kinds of evidence. Combining separate proportions assumes their remaining relationships can be ignored; there is no observed joint count for all filters.

Spreadsheet sources have sheets and cells rather than page numbers. Use the exact table references below with the retained workbook. Official pages may update; the retained file and fingerprint identify the version used.

Children and co-parents: evidence gap

Reviewed, not used as a multiplier. ONS cohort fertility describes children born to birth cohorts; it is not a current UK adult distribution of children and co-parents. The 2024 release says male cohort family-size distributions are unavailable. Household dependent-child counts also omit children living elsewhere or grown-up children.

No suitable co-parent-count distribution was verified for this calculator. Children preferences therefore remain visibly unavailable and do not change the percentage.
